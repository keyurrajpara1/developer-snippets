What is Alpine.js?
Alpine.js is a rugged, minimal framework for composing JavaScript behavior in your markup. It's like Tailwind for JavaScript.
How does it work?
Alpine.js offers you a declarative way to add JavaScript behavior to your markup. It's similar to Vue.js but more lightweight and requires no build step.
Why use Alpine.js?
It's perfect for adding simple interactivity to your site without the complexity of larger frameworks. It's lightweight, has no build step, and is easy to learn.
